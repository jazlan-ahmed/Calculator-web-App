# CalcX Pro - 50-in-1 Ultimate Calculator & Converter Hub

![CalcX Pro Suite](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tools-50%20Calculators%20%26%20Converters-6366f1?style=for-the-badge)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-emerald?style=for-the-badge)
![Deploy](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deploy-GitHub%20Pages-blue?style=for-the-badge)

**CalcX Pro** is a modern, responsive, professional-grade **All-in-One Calculator Hub** featuring **50 specialized calculators and converters**. Built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+), it operates entirely on the client side with **zero backend dependencies**, making it lightning-fast, mobile-friendly, and perfect for static hosting on GitHub Pages.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🔍 **Real-Time Sidebar Search**: Instantly filter through all 50 tools using the quick search bar.
- 📱 **Persistent Navigation**: Fixed desktop sidebar navigation & smooth collapsible overlay drawer on mobile.
- 📖 **Interactive Usage Guides**: Every calculator includes a dedicated **"How to Use"** guide complete with step-by-step instructions, mathematical formulas, and helpful tips.
- 💱 **Live Exchange Rates**: Currency converter fetches real-time exchange rates via ExchangeRate-API with instant fallback support.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Mode**: Built-in glassmorphic color themes for comfortable viewing in any lighting environment.
- 💾 **LocalStorage Memory**: Automatically remembers your active tool, calculations history, and custom settings.
- ⚡ **Zero Dependencies**: 100% pure Vanilla Web Stack (HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript).

## 🛠️ Complete 50-Tool Catalog

### 🧮 1. Math & General (8 Tools)
1. **Scientific & Basic Calculator**: Full scientific keypad (`sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `log`, `ln`, `√`, `x²`, `xʸ`, `π`, `e`) with memory registers (`MC`, `MR`, `M+`, `M-`, `MS`) and `DEG/RAD` toggle.
2. **Percentage Calculator**: Calculates X% of Y instantly.
3. **Average Calculator**: Computes Mean, Median, Count, and Sum for a dataset.
4. **Ratio Calculator**: Solves ratio proportions ($A : B = C : D$).
5. **Fraction Calculator**: Performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on fractions with simplified output.
6. **LCM & HCF Calculator**: Computes Least Common Multiple & Highest Common Factor (GCD).
7. **Prime Checker**: Checks whether an integer is prime or composite.
8. **Random Generator**: Produces random integers within custom min/max bounds.

### 🏦 2. Finance & Investments (9 Tools)
9. **Loan / EMI Calculator**: Monthly installment breakdown, total interest, and total payable amount.
10. **Mortgage Calculator**: Calculates estimated monthly home mortgage payments.
11. **Compound Interest**: Projects future investment growth with compound frequency calculations.
12. **Simple Interest**: Calculates basic accrued interest over time.
13. **Savings Calculator**: Estimates future growth based on recurring monthly contributions.
14. **Investment Return (CAGR)**: Computes Compound Annual Growth Rate over multi-year holding periods.
15. **Retirement Calculator**: Projects retirement nest egg savings based on current age and monthly deposits.
16. **Inflation Calculator**: Calculates future costs adjusted for annual inflation rates.
17. **Credit Card Payoff**: Estimates total months and interest required to eliminate card debt.

### 💼 3. Business & Commerce (10 Tools)
18. **Sales Tax Calculator**: Computes total price inclusive of sales tax.
19. **VAT / GST Calculator**: Calculates gross amounts including Value Added Tax / Goods & Services Tax.
20. **Tip Calculator**: Splits restaurant bills and calculates per-person tip amounts.
21. **Discount Calculator**: Computes final discounted prices and money saved.
22. **Profit Margin Calculator**: Calculates profit margin percentages based on cost and selling prices.
23. **Profit & Loss Calculator**: Generates net profit or loss statements.
24. **Commission Calculator**: Calculates earnings based on total sales volume and rate.
25. **Salary Calculator**: Converts gross annual salary into estimated monthly and weekly earnings.
26. **Hourly Wage Calculator**: Estimates annual earnings based on hourly rates and weekly hours.

### 🏋️ 4. Health & Fitness (9 Tools)
28. **BMI Calculator**: Metric (cm, kg) and Imperial (ft, in, lb) Body Mass Index score & health category indicator.

37. **GPA Calculator**: Calculates semester Grade Point Average on a 4.0 scale.
38. **CGPA Calculator**: Computes Cumulative GPA across multiple academic semesters.
39. **Date Difference**: Calculates exact days, weeks, and months between two calendar dates.
40. **Time Duration**: Calculates elapsed hours and minutes between start and end times.

### 📐 6. Converters (8 Tools)
43. **Unit Converter**: Multi-unit conversions for Length, Weight, Temperature, Speed, and Area.
44. **Currency Converter**: Real-time international currency conversions powered by live exchange rates.
45. **Data Storage**: Converts KB, MB, GB, and TB storage values.
46. **Volume Converter**: Converts Liters, Milliliters, and Gallons.
47. **Pressure Converter**: Converts Bar, PSI, and Pascal ($Pa$).
48. **Energy Converter**: Converts KiloCalories ($kcal$), Joules ($J$), and Kilowatt-Hours ($KWh$).
49. **Cooking Converter**: Converts Cups, Tablespoons ($tbsp$), and Teaspoons ($tsp$).
50. **History Log**: Keeps a rolling 30-entry log of past evaluations for quick reference.

## 🚀 Live Demo & Deployment

This repository includes a pre-configured **GitHub Actions workflow** (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) that automatically deploys the latest version of CalcX Pro to **GitHub Pages** whenever changes are pushed to `main`.

### Local Setup
To run CalcX Pro locally:
1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/jazlan-ahmed/Calculator-web-App.git
   ```
2. Open `index.html` in any web browser!
